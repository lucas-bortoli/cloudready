PRIVACY POLICY

CloudReady is deeply interested in your privacy, mostly because it is a very
private matter. Your local profile, display name, and chosen picture belong to
your little corner of this machine.

We may remember the things you ask CloudReady to remember so that it can
continue being useful. We do not peer over your shoulder, read your diary, or
form opinions about the naming conventions of your folders. A directory called
"final-final-really-final" is between you and history.

From time to time, CloudReady may store small pieces of information so the
world looks the same when you return. This is not surveillance. It is furniture
placement.

By continuing, you acknowledge that privacy is a shared project: we will mind
our business, and you will try not to paste secrets into places designed for
demonstrations. If something feels too personal, treat it as too personal.

This policy may evolve as CloudReady discovers new and interesting ways to be a
computer. Should that happen, we will endeavor to remain suitably [redacted].
